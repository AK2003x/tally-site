Tally privacy policy

Last updated October 2026

The short version. Tally keeps what you log on your iPhone. It has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics. We never see your data.

What Tally stores. The entries you log (amounts, names, categories, dates, who they were for), your wallets, budgets, recurring items and settings. All of it is saved in Tally's own storage on your device.

What leaves your device. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Tally itself makes no network connections; the only online step is the optional Tally Plus purchase, which goes through Apple.

Tally Plus. If you buy Tally Plus, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. Tally learns only whether Plus is unlocked. We never see your name, email or payment details.

Backups. If you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, Apple includes Tally's data in that backup, under your own Apple Account. If you use "Back up to Files" or "Export", the file goes wherever you choose to save or share it.

Face ID. If you turn on the lock, your iPhone checks your face, fingerprint or passcode and tells Tally only whether it matched. Tally never receives biometric data.

Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification service outside your iPhone is involved.

Deleting your data. Delete entries, wallets or anything else inside the app at any time. Deleting Tally from your iPhone removes everything it stored.

Children. Tally is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes. If a future version handles data differently, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Contact. To reach us, open a support request.
