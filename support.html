Support

Help with Tally for iPhone.

Get in touch. For a question, a problem or a suggestion, email hello.tallyapp@gmail.com. Please don't include amounts or anything else you'd rather keep private.

Where is my data? On your iPhone only. Tally has no account and no server, so we can't see or recover it. Use Settings, then Your data, to back it up to a file you keep.

Moving to a new iPhone. An iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone includes Tally's data. You can also back up to a file on the old phone and restore from it on the new one.

Tally Plus. A one-time purchase made through Apple. To get it back after reinstalling, open the Tally Plus sheet and tap Restore Purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Locked out. The lock uses your iPhone's own Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. If Face ID fails, your iPhone passcode unlocks Tally.
